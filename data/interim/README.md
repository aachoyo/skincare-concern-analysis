# Interim data

Generated by the pipeline; safe to delete and regenerate.

- `cleaned_cosmetics.csv` — output of `data_cleanup.py` (missing ingredient lists
  scraped from INCIdecoder filled in).
- `manual_review.csv` — rows the scraper could not resolve automatically.
- `manual_review_completed.csv` — the above after you fill ingredients by hand
  (input to `dataset_build.py`).
